Through the herbal planting programme run by Rideekanda Forest Monastery, we hope to conserve the valuable and increasingly rare medicinal plants that grow in Sri Lanka, and so protect species that are disappearing. In the same way, by planting the trees and herbs needed to prepare paṇḍu dye for robes, what is required for robe-making can be found easily within the monastery itself.

This planting programme also matters for passing a knowledge of medicinal plants to the coming generation, and for making those plants easy to find whenever they are needed.
