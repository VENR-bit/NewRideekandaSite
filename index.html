01The Monastery

A place for meditation in the stillness of the forest — preserving the true teaching of the Buddha and guiding seekers along the noble path.

A forest refuge for meditation and the Dhamma

Set on a quiet hillside at Udasgiriya in Matale, Rideekanda is a Theravāda forest monastery in the Sri Lankan forest tradition — a sanctuary of silence, simplicity, and sincere practice.

Monastics and lay practitioners come together here for residential retreats, daily meditation, walking meditation, chanting, and Dhamma discussion, surrounded by ancient forest, golden sunsets, and the stillness of the mountains.

02The 7-Day Residential Retreat

A progressive
journey to insight

Concentration & Vipassanā meditation, taught in the Sri Lankan Forest Tradition. The programme unfolds in three movements, each building on the last.

Keep scrolling
01

Concentration

samādhi · Ānāpānasati

Begin by developing deep concentration through breath meditation — calming the restless mind and exploring the jhāna states the Buddha himself used as the foundation for his awakening.

02

Body contemplation

arising · passing away

Learn to perceive the body not as a solid, permanent entity but as a dynamic interplay of fundamental energies, constantly arising and passing away — loosening the misconceptions of permanence, pleasure, and selfhood.

03

Vipassanā insight

udayabbaya-ñāṇa

Develop the wisdom of rising and falling through walking meditation and specialised breathing — perceiving directly the impermanence (anicca), unsatisfactoriness (dukkha), and non-self (anattā) of all things.

“Drop by drop is the water pot filled. Likewise, the wise person, gathering it little by little, fills oneself with good.”The Dhammapada

    05Life at the Retreat

    Simple, comfortable,
    arranged with care

    Accommodation

    Simple yet comfortable spaces with minimalist décor — private single rooms, private double rooms, and shared dormitories with individual beds.

    The dining experience

    Wholesome vegetarian meals prepared with locally-sourced ingredients. A full breakfast and lunch, with a light evening meal of soup or porridge.

    Guided practice

    Experienced meditation masters guide and support each practitioner, with one-to-one sessions to address individual needs and questions.

    06Our Teachers

    Years of practice in the
    remote forests

    Our programmes are designed and led by teachers with years of dedicated practice in the forests of Sri Pada, Anuradhapura, and Polonnaruwa.

    Meditation Teacher · Abbot

    Royal Paṇḍita Ven. Anuradhapura Ariyajeewa Thero

    Founder of the Ehipassiko International Meditation Center and a scholar of Sinhala, Pāli, and Sanskrit, he lived in the forests of Sri Pada, Anuradhapura, and Polonnaruwa for nearly five years — and teaches that emancipation is practically attainable.

    Meditation Teacher · Administrator

    Ven. Homagama Rewatha Thero

    With nearly a decade of meditation practice, he conducts weekly, monthly, and residential retreats for local and international practitioners — bridging ancient Buddhist wisdom with contemporary scientific perspectives.
